# What is Data Reality?

Data Reality is a Post‑Market Surveillance and Quality Event Monitoring platform.

It centralizes device event data, recall information, and regulatory workflows to help organizations maintain compliance and reduce risk.

## Primary goals

- Compliance and audit readiness: align with regulatory reporting requirements and maintain an auditable history.
- Risk mitigation: identify trends early and prioritize corrective actions.
- Operational insights: support Engineering, Quality, Safety, and Regulatory teams with actionable dashboards and exports.

## Who uses Data Reality?

- Quality and Safety teams monitoring events and injuries
- Regulatory teams preparing Vigilance, PSUR, or CRA documentation
- Product teams tracking malfunctions and corrective actions
