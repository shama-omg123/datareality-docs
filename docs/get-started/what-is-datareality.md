# What is Data Reality?

Data Reality is a Post‑Market Surveillance and Quality Event Monitoring platform.
It centralizes device event data, recall information, and regulatory workflows to help organizations maintain compliance and reduce risk.

![Data Reality Overview](/img/what-is-datareality-overview.png)

## Primary goals

- Compliance and audit readiness: align with regulatory reporting requirements and maintain an auditable history.
- Risk mitigation: identify trends early and prioritize corrective actions.
- Operational insights: support Engineering, Quality, Safety, and Regulatory teams with actionable dashboards and exports.

![Data Reality Goals](/img/what-is-datareality-goals.png)


## Who uses Data Reality?

- Quality and Safety teams monitoring events and injuries
- Regulatory teams preparing Vigilance, PSUR, or CRA documentation
- Product teams tracking malfunctions and corrective actions



![Data Reality Users](/img/what-is-datareality-users.png)

Additional Users and Fields:

* **Data Analysts** synthesizing complex datasets to identify emerging safety signals and quality trends.
* **Regulatory Compliance Officers** ensuring high-level organizational adherence to global standards (e.g., FDA, EMA, ISO).
* **Clinical Data Managers** maintaining data integrity and consistency across clinical study reports.
* **Medical Safety Officers** conducting medical assessments of reported adverse events and product complaints.
* **Quality Engineers** utilizing data to investigate root causes and drive continuous product improvement initiatives.
* **Risk Managers** leveraging aggregate data insights to assess and mitigate enterprise liability.
* **Internal and External Auditors** reviewing consolidated documentation trails in preparation for regulatory inspections.
* **Manufacturing Operations Leaders** monitoring production quality metrics to preemptively address line issues.
