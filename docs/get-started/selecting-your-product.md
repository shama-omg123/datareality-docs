# Selecting Your Product

Use the selector bar to switch between preferences such as `Starclose`, `Prostar`, `VIP`, `PerClose`, `Mynx`, and `SStplus`.

## Search

- Use the "Search preferences…" bar to quickly locate a product by name.
- Click the arrow controls to move to adjacent preferences without typing.
- Favorites: pin high‑priority preferences for one‑click access.
