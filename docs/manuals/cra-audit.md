# Compliance Risk Assessment (CRA) & Audit

## CRA (Compliance Risk Assessment)

- Assess risk against applicable regulations (e.g., EU MDR, FDA guidance).
- Map controls and evidence to regulatory clauses.
- Generate risk ratings and recommended actions.

## Audit Log

- Search by user, action, date, or entity.
- Filter and export the complete audit trail for external review.
- Maintain immutable records supporting inspections.
